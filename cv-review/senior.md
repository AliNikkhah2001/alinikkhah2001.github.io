Target postings: Senior AI Engineer, Tech Lead, AI Engineering Manager (technical track).

## ATS analysis

| Check | 1-page file | 2-page file |
|---|---|---|
| Parsing (contacts, sections) | Pass | Pass |
| Keyword coverage | ~51% | ~63% |
| Quantified metrics | 24 | 47 (highest) |
| Unique action verbs | 10 (incl. mentored, established) | 12 |
| Page count | True 1 page | Renders 3 pages (trim for strict limits) |

Leadership signals: roadmap ownership, team of 5, SLOs, mentoring, C-suite readouts, canary/auto-rollback governance. Keep people-leadership and technical-leadership claims in separate bullets so each is verifiable on its own.

## Market fit

Strongest proof: R&D roadmap + SLOs at marketplace scale, experimentation governance, cross-functional delivery with infra/product. Attach roadmap docs, SLO dashboards, and mentorship outcomes. Best for senior and lead postings.

## 1-page vs 2-page

True 1-page for screens and portals; trim the 2-page file to two pages before sending where limits are strict.
